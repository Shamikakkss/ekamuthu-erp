Core System Modules

Designed for Treasurers, Admins, and Community Members

Member & Role Management

Comprehensive registration tracking NIC details, phone numbers, and dependents with secure JWT multi-role permissions.

Automated Late Fine Engine

Automatically computes Rs. 100/- overdue fines per unpaid month with multi-month bulk subscription payment options.

Bank Slip Verification Portal

Self-service portal where members upload bank deposit receipts for instant Treasurer inspection and 1-click approvals.

Death Benefit Claim Workflow

Transparent claim submission and processing pipeline (Pending ➔ Approved ➔ Paid) for members and registered dependents.

PDF Receipt & Ledger Engine

Generates official downloadable PDF receipts with transaction reference IDs alongside exportable society financial summaries.

Financial Analytics

Aggregated real-time metrics tracking monthly subscription totals, outstanding dues, fine collections, and approved claims.
