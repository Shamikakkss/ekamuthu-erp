EkamuthuERP - Community Mutual Aid System

A comprehensive cloud-based ERP solution designed to digitize membership records, monthly contributions, automated late fine calculations, bank slip verification, and death benefit claim management for community mutual aid societies.

Explore Features GitHub Repository

Core System Modules

Powerful administrative tools engineered for smooth community society management

Members Directory & Role System

Maintain member NIC numbers, contact details, dependents list, and manage role-based access controls for Admins, Treasurers, and Members.

Automated Late Fine Calculation

Automatically calculates and appends late penalty fees (e.g. LKR 100/month) to monthly dues when payments exceed scheduled deadlines.

Bank Slip Upload & Verification

Members can upload payment receipts directly, allowing Treasurers and Admins to review, approve, or reject submissions seamlessly.

Real-Time Analytics Dashboard

Monitor society revenue trends, monthly collection progress, and financial statistics through interactive Recharts data visualizations.

Death Benefit Claims Management

Submit death benefit claims online and track payouts through a structured workflow: Pending ➔ Approved ➔ Disbursed.

PDF Receipts & Financial Reports

Generate instant printable PDF payment receipts for members alongside comprehensive financial summary reports for audit meetings.
