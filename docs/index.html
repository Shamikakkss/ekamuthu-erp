Core System Modules

Powerful digital tools engineered for transparent society administration and seamless member participation

Member & Dependent Directory

Comprehensive record keeping for member NICs, contact information, family dependent profiles, and granular Role-Based Access Control (Admin, Treasurer, Member).

Automated Late Fine Engine

Automated calculation and tagging of overdue fines (e.g., LKR 100/month) for payments made past the due date, seamlessly integrated into monthly account statements.

Bank Slip Upload & Verification

Members can upload digital deposit slips directly, enabling Admins and Treasurers to inspect, verify, approve, or reject payments in real time with audit trails.

Real-Time Financial Dashboard

Interactive income growth visualizer, monthly payment progress monitors, and overall society treasury analytics powered by responsive Recharts charts.

Death Benefit Claim Workflow

Streamlined claims submission for bereaved families, tracking benefits through a structured approval pipeline: Pending ➔ Approved ➔ Disbursed.

PDF Receipts & Audit Reports

Instant auto-generation of downloadable PDF payment receipts and comprehensive society-wide financial summary reports for monthly meetings.

Society Dues & Claim Process Workflow

5 simple steps from member registration to claim disbursement

1
Register Member
Create profile with NIC, contact numbers, and registered dependents
2
Upload Slip
Member submits monthly fee receipt or bank deposit slip online
3
Treasurer Audit
Treasurer reviews slip details and verifies incoming funds
4
Auto PDF Receipt
System updates payment status and issues an official PDF receipt
5
Claim Processing
Submit benefit claim upon bereavement for fast approval and payout
